R 163 | serves 4 | prep 15 | cook 30
T Rich, mild and creamy: the takeaway favourite at a fraction of the price.
I 600g | chicken thighs
I 150g | natural yoghurt
I 2 tbsp | tikka paste | c
I 1 | onion
I 2 cloves | garlic
I 3cm piece | fresh ginger
I 1 x 400g | tin chopped tomatoes
I 30g | butter | c
I 100ml | single cream
I 1 tsp | garam masala | c
I 300g | basmati rice | c
I 4 | naan breads
M Cut the chicken into chunks and mix with half the yoghurt and half the tikka paste. Leave for 10 minutes (or overnight in the fridge).
M Melt the butter and soften the chopped onion for 8 minutes. Add the garlic, grated ginger and the rest of the paste for 1 minute.
M Add the tomatoes and simmer for 10 minutes, then blend until smooth (or leave chunky).
M Add the chicken and cook gently for 15 minutes until cooked through. Stir in the cream, garam masala and the rest of the yoghurt.
M Serve with the rice and warm naan.

I 4 | pork sausages
I 6 | eggs
I 4 | tortilla wraps
I 80g | cheddar
I 4 tbsp | salsa
I 1 tsp | vegetable oil | c
M Squeeze the sausage meat from the skins and fry, breaking it up, until golden and cooked through.
M Add the beaten eggs and stir gently until just set.
M Fill the warm wraps with the sausage and egg, cheese and salsa, and roll up.
N Wrap in foil and freeze for grab-and-go mornings. Reheat thoroughly.

R 216 | serves 8 | prep 10 | cook 15

I 50g | butter | c
I 100g | cheddar
I 150ml | milk
I 1 tsp | mustard | c
M Heat the oven to 220C (200C fan). Rub the butter into the flour, then stir in most of the cheese and the mustard.

R 001 | serves 2 | prep 10 | cook 15
I 2 x 150g | black cod fillets, skin on (or any firm white fish)
I 2 tbsp | white miso paste
I 1 tbsp | honey | c
I 1 tbsp | rice vinegar | c
I 1 tsp | soy sauce | c
I 150g | jasmine rice | c
I 3cm piece | fresh ginger
I 2 | spring onions
I 1 tsp | sesame seeds | c
I 1 tsp | vegetable oil | c
M Mix the miso, honey, rice vinegar and soy sauce. Coat the fish and leave for 10 minutes (or up to a day in the fridge).
M Rinse the rice and cook with 300ml water and half the ginger, finely grated, for 12 minutes with the lid on, then rest for 5 minutes.
M Heat the grill to high. Wipe off the excess glaze, place the fish skin-side down on an oiled, foil-lined tray and grill for 8-10 minutes until caramelised and flaking.
M Cut the rest of the ginger into fine matchsticks and slice the spring onions.
M Serve the fish on the rice, topped with ginger, spring onion and sesame seeds.

R 002 | serves 4 | prep 15 | cook 30
I 300g | paella rice (or risotto rice) | c
I 1 pinch | saffron threads | c
I 900ml | hot fish or vegetable stock | c
I 1 | onion
I 1 | red pepper
I 3 cloves | garlic
I 1 tsp | smoked paprika | c
I 200g | raw king prawns
I 300g | mussels, cleaned (discard any open ones that don't close when tapped)
I 150g | squid rings
I 100g | frozen peas
I 2 | lemons
I 2 tbsp | olive oil | c
I 1 small bunch | flat-leaf parsley
M Soak the saffron in the hot stock. Dice the onion and pepper; slice the garlic.
M Heat the oil in a wide pan and soften the onion and pepper for 6 minutes. Add the garlic and paprika for 1 minute, then stir in the rice to coat.
M Pour in the saffron stock, stir once, then simmer without stirring for 15 minutes.
M Push the prawns, mussels and squid into the rice, scatter over the peas, cover and cook for 6-8 minutes until the mussels open and the prawns are pink. Discard any mussels that stay closed.
M Meanwhile, halve the lemons and char them cut-side down in a dry pan. Serve the paella with the charred lemon and chopped parsley.

R 003 | serves 2 | prep 15 | cook 50
I 2 x 150g | beef fillet steaks
I 2 | large floury potatoes (Maris Piper)
I 40g | butter
I 200ml | beef stock | c
I 2 tsp | black peppercorns, crushed | c
I 1 | shallot
I 2 tbsp | brandy (optional)
I 2 sprigs | thyme
I 1 tbsp | vegetable oil | c
I 150g | green beans
M Heat the oven to 200C (180C fan). Peel the potatoes and cut into thick cylinders, about 4cm tall.
M Brown the potatoes in half the butter and the oil for 4 minutes each side. Add half the stock and the thyme, then bake for 35-40 minutes until tender.
M Season the steaks and sear in a very hot pan for 3 minutes each side for medium-rare. Rest for 5 minutes.
M In the same pan, soften the finely chopped shallot, add the peppercorns and brandy, then the remaining stock. Reduce by half and whisk in the remaining butter.
M Boil the green beans for 4 minutes. Serve the steak with the fondant potatoes, beans and peppercorn jus.

R 004 | serves 2 | prep 15 | cook 30
I 2 | duck breasts
I 1 | small celeriac (about 500g)
I 150ml | milk
I 15g | butter
I 150g | cherries, fresh or frozen, stoned
I 100ml | red wine (or extra stock)
I 100ml | chicken stock | c
I 1 tsp | honey | c
I 100g | tenderstem broccoli
M Peel and chop the celeriac. Simmer in the milk with a splash of water for 20 minutes until soft, then blend with the butter and season.
M Score the duck skin, season, and place skin-side down in a cold pan. Cook over a medium heat for 10-12 minutes to render the fat, then turn and cook for 3-4 minutes. Rest for 5 minutes.
M Pour off most of the fat. Add the cherries, wine, stock and honey and simmer for 6-8 minutes until syrupy.
M Steam the broccoli for 4 minutes. Slice the duck and serve on the purée with the cherry sauce.

R 005 | serves 2 | prep 15 | cook 25
I 1 | lamb rack (6-8 bones)
I 40g | shelled pistachios
I 1 tbsp | Dijon mustard | c
I 1 small bunch | mint
I 120g | couscous | c
I 1 | lemon
I 2 tbsp | pomegranate seeds
I 1 tbsp | olive oil | c
I 1 | small cucumber
M Heat the oven to 200C (180C fan). Season the lamb and brown it all over in a hot pan.
M Finely chop the pistachios with half the mint. Brush the fat side of the lamb with mustard and press on the crust.
M Roast for 15-18 minutes for pink. Rest for 8 minutes.

R 006 | serves 2 | prep 10 | cook 30
I 180g | risotto rice | c
I 250g | mixed mushrooms
I 15g | dried porcini (optional)
I 1 | onion
I 2 cloves | garlic
I 750ml | hot vegetable stock | c
I 30g | vegetarian hard cheese, grated
I 20g | butter
I 1 tsp | truffle oil
I 8 | sage leaves
I 1 tbsp | olive oil | c
M Soak the porcini in 150ml of the hot stock, then chop and add the liquid back to the stock.
M Soften the chopped onion in half the butter and the oil for 5 minutes. Add the garlic and rice and stir for 1 minute.
M Add the stock a ladle at a time, stirring, for 18-20 minutes until the rice is creamy but still has a little bite.
M Meanwhile, fry the mushrooms in a hot pan until golden. Fry the sage leaves in a little oil until crisp.
M Stir the mushrooms, porcini, remaining butter and cheese into the risotto. Serve with a few drops of truffle oil and the crispy sage.

R 009 | serves 2 | prep 15 | cook 30
I 1 | large cauliflower
I 2 tbsp | harissa paste
I 2 tbsp | olive oil | c
I 3 tbsp | tahini
I 1 | lemon
I 1 clove | garlic
I 150g | basmati rice | c
I 30g | dried apricots or raisins
I 2 tbsp | pomegranate seeds
I 20g | toasted flaked almonds
I 1 small bunch | coriander or parsley
M Heat the oven to 220C (200C fan). Cut two thick steaks from the middle of the cauliflower (roast the rest alongside).
M Mix the harissa and oil, brush over the cauliflower and roast for 25-30 minutes, turning once, until charred and tender.

R 010 | serves 4 | prep 20 | cook 35
I 4 x 120g | skinless salmon fillets (or one 500g piece)
I 1 x 320g | ready-rolled puff pastry
I 100g | baby spinach
I 100g | soft cheese
I 1 | lemon
I 1 small bunch | dill
I 1 | egg, beaten
I 150g | crème fraîche
I 300g | new potatoes
M Heat the oven to 200C (180C fan). Wilt the spinach, squeeze out the water and mix with the soft cheese, lemon zest and half the chopped dill.
M Unroll the pastry, lay the salmon in the middle, top with the spinach mix and fold the pastry over to make a parcel. Seal, glaze with egg and score the top.

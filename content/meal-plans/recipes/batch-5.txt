R 141 | serves 4 | prep 10 | cook 35
I 500g | beef mince
I 1 | onion
I 1 | carrot
I 2 cloves | garlic
I 2 x 400g | tins chopped tomatoes
I 1 tbsp | tomato purée | c
I 1 | beef stock cube | c
I 1 tsp | dried mixed herbs | c
I 1 tbsp | vegetable oil | c
I 400g | spaghetti | c
I 30g | cheddar
M Fry the chopped onion and grated carrot in the oil for 5 minutes. Add the garlic for 1 minute.

R 143 | serves 4 | prep 15 | cook 45
I 500g | beef mince
I 1 | onion
I 2 | carrots
I 1 tbsp | plain flour | c
I 1 tbsp | tomato purée | c
I 1 tbsp | Worcestershire sauce | c
I 1 | beef stock cube | c
I 150g | frozen peas
I 1kg | potatoes
I 30g | butter | c
I 50ml | milk
I 1 tbsp | vegetable oil | c
M Heat the oven to 200C (180C fan). Peel the potatoes, cut into chunks and boil for 15-20 minutes until soft.
M Meanwhile fry the chopped onion and diced carrots in the oil for 5 minutes, then brown the mince.
M Stir in the flour and purée, then the Worcestershire sauce, crumbled stock cube and 300ml water. Simmer for 15 minutes, adding the peas for the last 2.
M Mash the potatoes with the butter and milk. Spoon the mince into a dish, top with the mash and bake for 20 minutes until golden.

R 145 | serves 4 | prep 10 | cook 35

R 148 | serves 4 | prep 15 | cook 120
I 600g | diced stewing beef
I 2 tbsp | plain flour | c
I 1 | onion
I 3 | carrots
I 500g | potatoes
I 1 | beef stock cube | c
I 1 tbsp | tomato purée | c
I 1 tsp | dried mixed herbs | c
I 2 tbsp | vegetable oil | c
I 4 slices | bread
M Heat the oven to 160C (140C fan). Toss the beef in the flour and brown it in batches in the oil in an ovenproof pan.
M Add the chopped onion and cook for 5 minutes. Add the carrots and potatoes in chunks, the purée, herbs, crumbled stock cube and 600ml water.
M Cover and cook in the oven for 2 hours until the beef is tender. Serve with bread.
N Also works in a slow cooker: 7-8 hours on low.

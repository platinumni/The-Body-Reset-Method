# Batch 7 (2026-10-27): cheap, simple snacks so the week's snack cupboard has variety. Written by Claude.

R 218 | serves 2 | prep 5 | cook 0
T A little cheeseboard: crackers, cheddar and sweet grapes.
I 8 | crackers
I 60g | cheddar
I 150g | grapes
M Slice the cheese and serve with the crackers and grapes.

R 219 | serves 2 | prep 5 | cook 0
T Banana rolled up in a peanut butter wrap and sliced into pinwheels.
I 1 | tortilla wraps
I 2 tbsp | peanut butter | c
I 1 | banana
M Spread the wrap with peanut butter, lay the banana at one edge and roll up tightly.
M Slice into rounds.

R 220 | serves 4 | prep 2 | cook 5
T Warm popcorn with a crunchy cinnamon-sugar coating.
I 60g | popcorn kernels
I 1 tbsp | vegetable oil | c
I 1 tbsp | sugar | c
I 1 tsp | ground cinnamon | c
M Heat the oil in a large lidded pan, add the kernels, cover and shake over a medium heat until the popping stops.
M Toss with the sugar and cinnamon while warm.

R 221 | serves 2 | prep 3 | cook 0
T Creamy yoghurt with a sweet jam swirl and berries.
I 250g | natural yoghurt
I 2 tbsp | jam | c
I 100g | frozen berries
M Spoon the yoghurt into two pots, swirl through the jam and top with berries (thawed or frozen).

R 222 | serves 2 | prep 3 | cook 0
T Crisp apple and tangy cheddar: a simple classic.
I 1 | apple
I 60g | cheddar
M Slice the apple and cheese and serve together.

R 223 | serves 4 | prep 5 | cook 10
T Crunchy homemade pitta chips with creamy hummus.
I 4 | pitta breads
I 1 tbsp | vegetable oil | c
I 1 tsp | smoked paprika | c
I 200g | hummus
M Heat the oven to 200C (180C fan). Cut the pittas into triangles, toss with the oil and paprika and bake for 8-10 minutes until crisp.
M Serve with the hummus.

R 224 | serves 4 | prep 10 | cook 0

I 2 | apples
I 2 | bananas
I 2 | oranges
I 150g | grapes
M Chop the fruit, mix with any juice from the oranges and share into cups.

R 225 | serves 2 | prep 2 | cook 3

I 1 tbsp | honey | c

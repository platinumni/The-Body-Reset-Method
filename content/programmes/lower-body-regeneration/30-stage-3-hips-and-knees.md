Stage 3 moves up to the hips and knees. Your knees largely do what your feet and hips tell them to, so this stage connects the two.

You'll begin with two simple floor exercises for the glutes and the side of the hip. Then you'll take everything you've learned back onto one leg, with a hip hinge and a split squat.

> A stable hip hinge with almost no range of motion beats going all the way and compensating. This applies to every movement.

Most of the support still comes from the ground. Keep your feet alive and responsive: engage, but don't grip.

> Keep what the movement needs. Let go of everything else.

- **Recognise:** notice where you grip, brace and compensate, and which muscles should be doing the work.
- **Release:** let go of the compensation first, then the excess effort in the muscles you need. Find the balance point.
- **Reconnect:** feel each part of the movement: pressure, timing, which muscles switch on.
- **Retrain and strengthen:** only once the foundations feel right.
- **Integrate:** at advanced levels, keep movements simple and controlled while your mind is busy (breathing, counting).

**Three questions for every exercise:**

1. What am I gripping or forcing?
2. Which muscles should be working?
3. Can I do this with less effort and still stay stable?

Go at your own pace. Stop if anything causes pain.

You've finished the core programme. From here you can simply go and live your life, carrying this awareness into everything you do. Or, if you want to keep building, here are some simple bodyweight exercises to add.

These don't come with the full detailed guidance. By now you know how to approach them: find the position, release what isn't needed, engage but don't grip, and keep position over range.

## Bodyweight

- **Knees-over-toes lunges:** a long lunge where the front knee travels forward over the toes, heel staying down. Start small.
- **Split squats:** like the Bulgarian split squat, but with the back foot on the floor.
- **Reverse lunges:** step back into a lunge, then return. Easier on the knees than stepping forward.
- **Step-ups:** onto a step or stair, slowly, with the front foot doing the work.
- **Single-leg calf raises** on a step.

## At home with a resistance band

- **Banded glute bridges:** band just above the knees.
- **Banded side steps:** band around the ankles or just above the knees, small steps sideways.
- **Banded clamshells.**
- **Banded tibialis raises:** band around the front of the foot, anchored low in front of you.

## Taking it into your own training and sport

- Warm up with a short round: short foot, single-leg balance and pogo hops are enough.
- In any exercise, bring back the same three questions: What am I gripping or forcing? Which muscles should be working? Can I do this with less effort and still stay stable?
- When you add load or speed, expect the old habits to come back for a while. Allow it, then release what isn't needed, as you've practised.
- Everything starts at the feet. Keep them alive and responsive in whatever you do.

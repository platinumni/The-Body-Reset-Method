## Starting position
- Lie on your side with your hips and knees bent, heels together and in line with your bottom. Rest your head on your arm or a cushion.
- **Stack your hips** softly one above the other, and your shoulders the same. A gentle nudge into place, then release.
- Release the neck, shoulders and stomach. Breathe into the upper side of the ribs.

## Conducting the exercise
- Keeping your heels together, **lift the top knee**, like a clam opening.
- Open only as far as you can without your pelvis rolling backwards.
- Pause, then close slowly.
- Feel the work at the side and back of the top hip.

## What to watch for
- **The pelvis rolling back** to make the movement look bigger. This is the most common one.
- **The lower back arching.**
- **Pressing hard with the top hand** on the floor, or tensing the neck.
- **Rushing** the closing phase.

## Easier and harder versions
### Beginner
A small opening, with your back against a wall to help keep the hips stacked.

### Intermediate
The full clamshell, as above, with a pause at the top.

### Advanced
A resistance band just above the knees. Or lift the feet slightly off the floor while you open and close. With more load, the pelvis will try to roll and the breath to hold. Stay small, stay stacked, keep breathing.

## Key points
- Hips stacked, heels together.
- Open the knee; the pelvis stays still.
- Pause, close slowly.

Welcome to Lower Body Regeneration. Before you try a single exercise, read this section all the way through, and come back to it more than once. The exercises only work properly when you understand the thinking behind them.

## Most of us were never taught how to move

Over years of sitting, rushing, training hard and pushing through, the body learns to protect itself. It grips through the feet, braces through the knees and tightens around the hips. It happens so gradually that most people never realise they're doing it.

That holding comes at a cost. When muscles are always working to keep you safe, they can't do the job they're there for. Certain positions stop being available to you, simple movements start to feel like effort, and you never quite trust yourself in a balanced position. Training harder on top of that only makes the pattern stronger.

> Strength built on top of tension and compensation simply makes the compensation stronger.

## This programme works the other way round

First, you learn to recognise where you're gripping and forcing, and which muscles should really be doing the work. Then you learn to let go of the effort you don't need, without losing the stability you do need. Only then do you rebuild coordination, strength and balance, on a foundation you can trust.

The aim isn't to force your body into better positions. It's to release what's been holding it back, and to learn to love and trust your body again.

## Why we start at the feet

Your feet are the foundation for every step you take. When your arches and toes aren't doing their job, the ankles, knees and hips above them have to compensate. So this programme starts at the ground and works upwards, one stage at a time:

1. Feet, arches and toes
2. Achilles and calves
3. Knees
4. Hips

Because everything above is built on what happens below, the work you do at the feet shapes how your whole body stands, walks and moves.

## How to use this programme

- **Read the Full Programme first**, ideally a few times, until the ideas make sense to you.
- **Then train with the Quick Run-Through** beside you. It has the key points of each stage.
- **Go at your own pace.** Moving on too early only brings the old gripping back.
- **Stop if anything causes pain**, and get advice before continuing. This is education and movement coaching, not medical treatment.

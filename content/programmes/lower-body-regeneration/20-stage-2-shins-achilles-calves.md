Stage 2 begins the foundational strengthening of the lower leg: the front of the shin, the Achilles and the calves.

You're still using everything from stage 1. The difference is that there's now more load, so there's more for your body to grip and compensate with. That's useful. It shows you, very clearly, where you're holding.

Remember: we're building **strength in the position you have.** Full range isn't needed at first.

## Starting position
- Exactly the same as part 1: two feet, soft knees, tension released everywhere, breathing easily.
- This time, **don't consciously grip the toes.**

## Conducting the exercise
- Just bounce, and **trust your body to do it.**
- Stay loose. Let your feet and toes respond on their own.
- If you notice yourself taking control again, smile, release, and carry on.
- This is the goal of the whole programme: your body doing the right thing without you having to think about it.

## What to watch for
- **Taking back control** without noticing: the toes clenching, the bounce getting stiff.
- **Bouncing too high** too soon.
- **Holding the breath.**

## Easier and harder versions
### Beginner
Hands on the kitchen counter, small bounces, just letting go.

### Intermediate
The free bounce, as above.

### Advanced
The boxer skip and higher bounces, all while staying released and trusting your reflexes.

## Key points
- Same bounce. No conscious toe grip.
- Stay loose and trust your body.
- This is the goal: unconsciously competent.

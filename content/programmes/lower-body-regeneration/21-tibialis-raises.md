## Starting position
- Stand barefoot with your back and bottom against a wall, and walk your heels about a foot's length away from it.
- Feet hip-width apart, ankles, knees and hips in line. Soft knees, not locked.
- Let the wall take your weight so you don't need to brace. Ribs stacked, shoulders loose, jaw soft.
- Breathe 360° into the ribs and release whatever you can before you move.

## Conducting the exercise
- Keeping your heels on the floor, **lift your toes and the front of your feet** towards your shins.
- Pause for a moment at the top, then lower slowly.
- The work is at the front of the shin. Everything else stays quiet.

## What to watch for
- **Pushing the hips forward** off the wall, or arching the back.
- **Toes clawing upwards** while the foot itself barely lifts. Let the whole front of the foot come up.
- **Ribs flaring** and the breath stopping.
- **Rolling onto the outside edges** of the feet.

## Easier and harder versions
### Beginner
Seated on a hard chair: lift the front of the feet, keeping the heels down.

### Intermediate
Against the wall, as above.

### Advanced
Walk the heels further from the wall for more load. Or slow everything down: 1 second up, a 2-second pause, 3 seconds down, while you keep breathing and released.

## Key points
- Back on the wall, heels forward, soft knees.
- Front of the foot up, pause, slowly down.
- Only the shins work.

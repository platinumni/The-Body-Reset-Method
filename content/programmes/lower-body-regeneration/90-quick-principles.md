> Release the tension. Find the muscles that should be working. Then release even those. Trust your reflexes.

- **Position over range of motion.** Build strength in the position you've got.
- **Never force into a position that makes you compensate.**
- **Engage, but don't grip.**
- **A little shaking is normal.** Stop if pain goes above 2–3 out of 10.
- **Don't rush to the advanced versions.**

**Three questions for every exercise:**

1. What am I gripping or forcing?
2. Which muscles should be working?
3. Can I do this with less effort and still stay stable?

**Advised:** one round a day, about 8–10 reps of each. Not every day is needed.

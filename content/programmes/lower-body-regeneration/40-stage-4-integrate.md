This is the culmination of the programme. Everything you've practised comes together in one simple, rhythmic movement: the pogo hop.

It comes in two parts, and they mirror the last two stages of learning:

- **Part 1 is consciously competent.** You keep a little conscious control in the toes, so you know they're there.
- **Part 2 is unconsciously competent.** You let go of that conscious control and trust your body to do it.

It's also a lovely, rhythmic way to loosen up and release tension at the end of a round.

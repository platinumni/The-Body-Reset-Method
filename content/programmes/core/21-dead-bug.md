## Starting position
- Start in the 3-month position: on your back, hips and knees at 90°, legs up.
- Reach both arms straight up towards the ceiling.
- Breathe 360°, wide into the waist. No bracing. Find the balance point.
- **Settle in softly:** nudge gently into position, then release. Engage only what's needed, release again, and maintain. When it's right, it feels light, almost floating.

## Conducting the exercise
- Slowly lower **one arm** back overhead and the **opposite leg** out towards the floor.
- Only go as far as your back and ribs stay exactly the same.
- Bring them back, and swap sides.
- Keep breathing throughout.

## What to watch for
- **The lower back arching** away from the floor.
- **The ribs flaring** as the arm goes back.
- **Holding the breath.**
- **Rushing.**

## Easier and harder versions
### Beginner
Move only the legs, one at a time, tapping a heel to the floor, with the arms resting.

### Intermediate
Opposite arm and leg, as above.

### Advanced
**Cross-body dead bug:** press the left knee into the right hand (or elbow) and hold that press, while the other arm and leg slowly extend and stay released. Swap sides. Or hold a yoga ball between your hands and knees and press gently into it while you move the other limbs.

The cross-body version asks one side to press while the other stays released, which is a genuine test of control. Expect the breath to hold and the lower back to lift; slow down until neither happens.

## Key points
- Legs at 90°, arms up.
- Opposite arm and leg away, slowly.
- Back and ribs stay the same.
- Breathe.

## Starting position
- Lie on your side with your knees and hips bent, head resting on your arm or a cushion.
- Stack your hips and shoulders. Release the neck, jaw and shoulders.

## Conducting the exercise
- Breathe gently into the **top side of your ribs.** Feel them widen and lift with the in-breath.
- As you breathe out, slowly **reach your top arm** up and over your head, opening the side of the ribs.
- Bring it back as you breathe in. Easy and slow.

## What to watch for
- **Rolling backwards** as the arm reaches.
- **The lower back arching.**
- **Forcing the reach.**

## Easier and harder versions
### Beginner
Just breathe into the top ribs, without the reach.

### Intermediate
Breath and reach, as above.

### Advanced
Hold the reach overhead for 3 slow breaths, keeping everything else soft.

## Key points
- On your side, hips stacked.
- Breathe into the top ribs.
- Reach overhead on the out-breath.

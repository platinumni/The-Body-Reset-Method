## Starting position
- Lie face down with your forehead resting on your stacked hands, legs relaxed and toes turned slightly in or out, whatever is comfortable.
- Let your whole body sink into the floor. Release the jaw, shoulders, glutes and legs.

## Conducting the exercise
- Breathe in gently through your nose. Feel your stomach press into the floor, and your **lower back and sides** rise and widen.
- Breathe out slowly and let everything soften.
- The breath goes 360°: front, sides and back. Not a big breath, just an easy, wide one.

## What to watch for
- **Breathing only into the chest** and shoulders.
- **Forcing** big breaths.
- **Tensing the glutes** or the lower back.

## Easier and harder versions
### Beginner
Just lie and breathe, noticing where the breath goes.

### Intermediate
Direct the breath into the lower back and sides.

### Advanced
Lengthen the out-breath, for example 4 seconds in and 6 seconds out, without any tension creeping in.

## Key points
- Face down, forehead on your hands.
- Breathe low and wide: front, sides and back.
- Easy breaths, no forcing.

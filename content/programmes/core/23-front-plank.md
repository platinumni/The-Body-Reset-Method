## Starting position
- Lie face down with your forearms on the floor, elbows under your shoulders.
- Tuck your toes under.
- Breathe 360° before you lift, and release any bracing.

## Conducting the exercise
- Lift your body into a straight line from your head to your heels.
- Hold it **released:** only as much effort as you need to stay in the line, breathing easily.
- At the end of the hold, add a short **squeeze:** glutes, legs and stomach working together, for a few seconds. Then lower down.
- The released plank comes first. The squeeze is the end point.

## What to watch for
- **The hips sagging** or lifting high.
- **Holding the breath.**
- **The shoulders shrugging** up to the ears.
- **Squeezing from the start.**

## Easier and harder versions
### Beginner
Knees on the floor, body in a line from the head to the knees.

### Intermediate
The full plank, as above.

### Advanced
Forearms on a yoga ball. Or lift one foot a little off the floor, keeping the hips level.

## Key points
- Forearms down, body in a line.
- Released first, breathing.
- A short squeeze at the end.

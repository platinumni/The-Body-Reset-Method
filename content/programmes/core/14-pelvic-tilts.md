## Starting position
- Lie on your back with your knees bent and feet flat, hip-width apart.
- Arms relaxed. Let the lower back rest in its natural curve.

## Conducting the exercise
- Gently **tilt the pelvis back**, so the lower back flattens towards the floor.
- Then **tilt it forward**, so the lower back arches slightly away.
- Move slowly between the two, making the movement smaller each time, until you **settle in the middle.** That's your neutral.
- Breathe throughout. The movement comes from the pelvis, not from pushing with the legs.

## What to watch for
- **Pushing through the feet** or squeezing the glutes to move.
- **Big, forced movements.**
- **Holding the breath.**

## Easier and harder versions
### Beginner
Very small tilts, with your hands on your hip bones to feel the movement.

### Intermediate
As above, finding neutral each time.

### Advanced
Do it seated on a hard chair, then standing, finding the same neutral.

## Key points
- Knees bent, feet flat.
- Tilt back, tilt forward, settle in the middle.
- Small, slow, breathing.

Well done. Keep a round in your week whenever you want it, and carry the feeling into everything else you do: support that's there when you need it, without bracing or gripping.

## You're meant to outgrow this

There's a phase of teaching the body, which might be a week or two for some people and months for others. Once you genuinely have the control, you can let go of most of the structure. A few minutes of the movements you find most useful, done with real awareness, will serve you better than a long session on autopilot.

The same concept lives everywhere: in the way you walk, lift your children or grandchildren, swing a golf club or train a martial art. Release what isn't needed, engage what is, and trust your body.

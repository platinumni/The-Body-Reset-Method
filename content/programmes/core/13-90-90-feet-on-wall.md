## Starting position
- Lie on your back with your feet flat on a wall, hips and knees bent at 90°.
- Arms relaxed by your sides, or one hand on your chest and one on your stomach.
- Release the neck, jaw, shoulders and stomach.

## Conducting the exercise
- Press **lightly** through your heels into the wall. Just enough to feel the backs of your legs switch on.
- Breathe gently 360°: into the front, sides and back of the ribs.
- Hold the light press and keep breathing. Then release the press a little more, and see if you can still feel it.
- This is the method in miniature: find the muscles, then release even those.

## What to watch for
- **Pressing too hard** into the wall.
- **Lifting the hips** or arching the back.
- **The breath going into the chest** and shoulders.

## Easier and harder versions
### Beginner
Feet on the wall, just breathing, with no press.

### Intermediate
A light heel press, as above.

### Advanced
Reach one arm overhead as you breathe out, keeping the ribs down. Or lift the hips a centimetre off the floor while you breathe.

## Key points
- Feet on the wall, 90° at hips and knees.
- Light heel press, then release a little more.
- Breathe 360°.

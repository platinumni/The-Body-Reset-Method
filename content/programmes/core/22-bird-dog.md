## Starting position
- Start on all fours as in quadruped rocking: hands under shoulders, knees under hips, spine long.
- Breathe 360°. Release the neck and shoulders.
- **Settle in softly:** nudge gently into position, then release. Engage only what's needed, release again, and maintain. When it's right, it feels light, almost floating.

## Conducting the exercise
- Slowly reach **one arm forward** and the **opposite leg back,** until they're in line with your body.
- Keep the hips and shoulders level, as if balancing a glass of water on your back.
- Pause and breathe, then bring them back and swap sides.

## What to watch for
- **The hips tipping** or rotating as the leg lifts.
- **Arching the lower back** to lift the leg higher.
- **The head dropping** or lifting.

## Easier and harder versions
### Beginner
Move only one limb at a time: one arm, then one leg.

### Intermediate
Opposite arm and leg, as above.

### Advanced
Do it with your body supported over a yoga ball. Or bring the elbow and knee together under the body before each reach.

On the ball, everything becomes less stable, and the body will grip to compensate. Keep the reach small, the breath easy and the hips level.

## Key points
- All fours, spine long.
- Opposite arm and leg out.
- Hips level. No tipping.

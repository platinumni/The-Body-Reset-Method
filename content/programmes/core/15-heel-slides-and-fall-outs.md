## Starting position
- Lie on your back in neutral, knees bent, feet flat.
- Breathe 360° and release any bracing in the stomach.
- **Settle in softly:** nudge gently into position, then release. Engage only what's needed, release again, and maintain. When it's right, it feels light, almost floating.

## Conducting the exercise
- **Heel slides:** slowly slide one heel along the floor until the leg is nearly straight, then slide it back. Then the other leg.
- **Knee fall-outs:** let one knee slowly fall out to the side, only as far as the pelvis stays still, then bring it back. Then the other knee.
- Keep breathing. The deep core keeps the pelvis quiet without you bracing.

## What to watch for
- **The pelvis rocking** or tipping as the leg moves.
- **The lower back** arching or pressing down.
- **Bracing the stomach** hard.

## Easier and harder versions
### Beginner
Small slides and small fall-outs.

### Intermediate
Fuller movements, as above.

### Advanced
Start from the 3-month position (legs up at 90°) and slowly lower and extend one leg at a time.

Moving the legs from the 3-month position asks the deep core to work while the limbs move, which is exactly where people start to brace. Breathe, and keep the pelvis quiet without gripping.

## Key points
- Neutral, breathing.
- Slide one heel away and back.
- Let one knee fall out and back.
- Pelvis stays still.

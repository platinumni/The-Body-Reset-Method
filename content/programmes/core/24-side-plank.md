## Starting position
- Lie on your side with your elbow directly under your shoulder and your legs stacked (or knees bent for the easier version).
- Stack your hips and shoulders. Breathe 360° before you lift.

## Conducting the exercise
- Lift your hips so your body is in a line from your head to your feet (or knees).
- Hold it released, with only the effort you need, breathing easily.
- Lower slowly and swap sides.

## What to watch for
- **The hips sagging** or rolling back.
- **The shoulder** sinking or shrugging.
- **Holding the breath.**

## Easier and harder versions
### Beginner
Knees bent, lifting from the knees.

### Intermediate
Legs straight, as above.

### Advanced
Lift the top leg. Or reach the top arm up to the ceiling and slowly thread it under your body and back.

## Key points
- Elbow under shoulder, hips stacked.
- Lift into a line.
- Released, breathing.

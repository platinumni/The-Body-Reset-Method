## Starting position
- Come onto all fours, hands under shoulders and knees under hips.
- Let your spine be long and natural, neck in line, head neither dropped nor lifted.
- Spread the fingers gently. Breathe 360°.
- **Settle in softly:** nudge gently into position, then release. Engage only what's needed, release again, and maintain. When it's right, it feels light, almost floating.

## Conducting the exercise
- Slowly **rock your hips back** towards your heels, then forward again over your hands.
- Keep the spine the same shape throughout. Only go back as far as it stays long.
- Smooth, slow and easy, breathing all the way.

## What to watch for
- **The lower back rounding** at the back of the rock.
- **The head dropping** or lifting.
- **Locking the elbows** or sinking into the shoulders.

## Easier and harder versions
### Beginner
Small rocks, with a cushion under the knees.

### Intermediate
Fuller rocks, as above.

### Advanced
Tuck the toes under and lift the knees a centimetre off the floor (a bear hover). Hold for a few breaths, released but deep.

Hovering the knees adds load, and the breath often stops the moment you lift. If it does, lower down, breathe, and try again more softly.

## Key points
- All fours, spine long.
- Rock back and forward.
- The spine doesn't change.

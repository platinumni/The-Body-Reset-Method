## Starting position
- Lie on your side with your bottom knee bent for support and your top leg straight.
- **Stack the hips.** Bring the top leg slightly behind the line of your body, toes facing forward, not up.
- Release the neck and shoulders, and breathe into the upper ribs.

## Conducting the exercise
- Lift the top leg a little way, leading with the heel.
- Pause, then lower slowly.
- Feel the work at the side of the hip, not the front of the thigh.

## What to watch for
- **The hips rolling back** and the toes turning up to the ceiling.
- **The leg drifting forward.**
- **Lifting too high** and hitching through the waist.

## Easier and harder versions
### Beginner
A small lift with your back against a wall to keep the hips stacked.

### Intermediate
As above, with a pause at the top.

### Advanced
A resistance band around the ankles, or a light ankle weight.

## Key points
- Hips stacked, leg slightly back.
- Lead with the heel.
- Small, slow and clean.

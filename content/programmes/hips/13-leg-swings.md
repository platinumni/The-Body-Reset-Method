## Starting position
- Stand side-on to a wall, holding it lightly with one hand.
- Stand tall on the inside leg, soft knee, with the foot alive but not gripping.
- Let the outside leg hang loose from the hip.
- **Settle in softly:** nudge gently into position, then release. Engage only what's needed, release again, and maintain. When it's right, it feels light, almost floating.

## Conducting the exercise
- **Front to back:** let the leg swing forward and back, like a pendulum. Start small and let it grow.
- **Side to side:** face the wall, holding it with both hands, and swing the leg across your body and out to the side.
- **360°:** when you're ready, let the leg trace big, easy circles in both directions.
- Stay relaxed. The swing should feel loose, not forced.

## What to watch for
- **The upper body swinging** with the leg.
- **Arching the lower back** on the backswing.
- **Forcing the height** of the swing.
- **The standing foot** clawing at the floor.

## Easier and harder versions
### Beginner
Small, slow swings, holding on with both hands.

### Intermediate
Fuller swings in all three directions.

### Advanced
Hands off the wall, balancing on the standing leg while the other swings freely.

Balancing while the other leg swings freely is real multi-tasking. Expect the standing foot to clench and the breath to hold; release them and let the swing stay loose.

## Key points
- Hold lightly, stand tall.
- Loose swings: front to back, side to side, then circles.
- The body stays still; the leg swings.

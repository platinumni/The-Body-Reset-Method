## Starting position
- Stand facing a wall or chair, holding it with both hands.
- Find your single-leg balance on one leg: ankle, knee and hip in line, soft knee, toes alive but not gripping.
- Hinge forward slightly at the hip, as in a single-leg Romanian deadlift, with the free leg reaching back.

## Conducting the exercise
- Keeping the standing leg still, slowly **rotate your pelvis open,** turning the free hip up towards the ceiling.
- Then rotate it slowly **closed,** turning the free hip down towards the floor.
- The movement happens around the standing hip. Smooth, slow and controlled.

## What to watch for
- **The standing knee** rolling in or locking.
- **Twisting the upper body** instead of the pelvis.
- **Gripping the wall** or the toes.
- **Holding the breath.**

## Easier and harder versions
### Beginner
A small hinge and small rotations, holding the wall with both hands.

### Intermediate
A deeper hinge and fuller rotations, holding with one hand.

### Advanced
No hands. Or hold a light weight.

## Key points
- Single-leg hinge, holding the wall.
- Rotate the pelvis open, then closed.
- The standing leg stays still.

## Starting position
- Sit on the floor with your front leg bent at 90° in front of you and your back leg bent at 90° out to the side. Both knees on, or close to, the floor.
- Place your hands on the floor behind you, on your knuckles or fingertips.
- **Push up off your knuckles** to sit tall, and let your body relax into the position. Don't force the knees down.
- Release your shoulders and jaw, and breathe into your ribs.
- **Settle in softly:** nudge gently into position, then release. Engage only what's needed, release again, and maintain. When it's right, it feels light, almost floating.

## Conducting the exercise
- Keeping your feet roughly where they are, **rotate both knees** up and over to the other side, so you end up in the mirror-image position.
- Pause, sit tall off the knuckles, relax into it, and breathe.
- Switch back. Slowly and smoothly.

## What to watch for
- **Leaning back** heavily onto the hands, or slumping the spine.
- **Forcing the knees** down to the floor.
- **Swinging** through the movement with momentum.
- **Holding the breath** at the hard part of the switch.

## Easier and harder versions
### Beginner
Keep your hands behind you for support, and only go as far as is comfortable. Sit on a cushion if your hips are tight.

### Intermediate
Lighter hand support: push up off the knuckles just enough to sit tall.

### Advanced
No hands. Arms out in front or crossed over your chest, sitting tall through each switch.

Without your hands, the body will reach for help: the spine slumps, the breath holds, the hips grip to haul you round. Move only as far as you can stay tall and released.

## Key points
- Both knees at 90°.
- Push up off the knuckles, then relax into it.
- Rotate slowly to the other side.
- Don't force the knees down.

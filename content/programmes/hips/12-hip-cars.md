## Starting position
- Stand tall side-on to a wall or chair, holding it lightly.
- Find your single-leg balance on the standing leg: ankle, knee and hip in line, soft knee, toes alive but not gripping.
- Ribs stacked over the pelvis. Release the jaw, shoulders and breath.

## Conducting the exercise
- Lift the outside knee up in front of you.
- Slowly draw a **circle** with the knee: out to the side, round behind you, and back down. Then reverse it.
- The rest of the body stays still. Only the hip moves.
- Keep the circles small at first. Range is built off the correct pattern, not by reaching for it.

CARs stands for controlled articular rotations: slow, controlled circles of a joint.

## What to watch for
- **Leaning** or tipping the upper body to make the circle bigger. This is the main one: no leaning.
- **Twisting through the lower back.**
- **Gripping the wall** hard.
- **The standing knee** locking or rolling in.

## Easier and harder versions
### Beginner
Very small circles, holding the wall with both hands.

### Intermediate
Bigger circles, as long as nothing else moves.

### Advanced
Let go of the wall. Or do them on all fours, which is harder to cheat.

## Key points
- Stand tall, hold lightly.
- Slow circles of the knee.
- No leaning. Small circles first.

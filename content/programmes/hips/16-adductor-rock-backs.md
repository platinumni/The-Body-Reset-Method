## Starting position
- Come onto all fours, hands under shoulders and knees under hips.
- Straighten one leg out to the side, foot flat on the floor, in line with the knee on the floor.
- Let the spine be long and natural, neither arched nor rounded. Release the neck and breathe.

## Conducting the exercise
- **Rock your hips back** towards your heels until you feel a stretch along the inner thigh of the straight leg.
- Keep the spine long. Only go as far as you can without it rounding.
- Rock slowly forward again. Smooth and easy.

## What to watch for
- **The lower back rounding** at the end of the rock.
- **Forcing the range.**
- **Holding the breath.**

## Easier and harder versions
### Beginner
Bend the outside knee slightly and keep the rocks small.

### Intermediate
Straight leg, as above.

### Advanced
Pause at the back of each rock for a few breaths, staying long and released.

## Key points
- All fours, one leg out to the side.
- Rock back, spine long.
- Only as far as it stays long.

## Starting position
- Lie on your back with both knees bent and feet flat.
- Cross one ankle over the opposite knee, making a figure 4.
- Let your head, neck and shoulders rest on the floor. Breathe into the ribs.
- **Settle in softly:** nudge gently into position, then release. Engage only what's needed, release again, and maintain. When it's right, it feels light, almost floating.

## Conducting the exercise
- Lift the bottom foot off the floor and gently draw both legs towards you, holding behind the thigh or the shin.
- Stop when you feel a stretch in the back and side of the hip of the crossed leg.
- Stay and breathe. Let the hip soften rather than pulling harder.

## What to watch for
- **Lifting the head** and tensing the neck and shoulders.
- **Pulling hard** with the arms.
- **The hips twisting** off the floor.

## Easier and harder versions
### Beginner
Keep the bottom foot on the floor, or against a wall.

### Intermediate
Draw the legs in, as above.

### Advanced
Seated version: sit tall on a chair or the floor and gently hinge forward from the hips, without rounding the spine.

Seated, there's nothing supporting your spine, so notice how quickly the back rounds and the shoulders tense. Hinge only as far as you stay long.

## Key points
- Ankle over the opposite knee.
- Draw in gently. Head and shoulders relaxed.
- Breathe, don't pull.

## Starting position
- Place a resistance band around your ankles (or just above the knees, which is easier).
- Stand with your feet hip-width apart, knees softly bent, weight spread across both feet.
- Stand tall, ribs stacked. Release the jaw, shoulders and breath.
- **Settle in softly:** nudge gently into position, then release. Engage only what's needed, release again, and maintain. When it's right, it feels light, almost floating.

## Conducting the exercise
- Take a **small step sideways,** then bring the other foot in, keeping some tension on the band.
- Keep your feet pointing forward and your body level.
- Step one way, then come back the other way.

## What to watch for
- **Leaning or rocking** the body side to side.
- **The knees caving in.**
- **The feet turning out.**
- **Taking big steps** and losing control.

## Easier and harder versions
### Beginner
No band: just the slow side steps with soft knees, focusing on staying level. Or band above the knees.

### Intermediate
Band around the ankles, as above.

### Advanced
A stronger band, or a slightly deeper knee bend.

A stronger band tempts the body to rock and the knees to cave. Slow down, stay tall and keep the steps small.

## Key points
- Band on, soft knees, tall.
- Small side steps, feet forward.
- No rocking.

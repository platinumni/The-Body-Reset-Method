## Starting position
- Kneel on one knee with a cushion under it, the other foot flat in front of you, both knees at about 90°.
- Stack your ribs over your pelvis. Gently **tuck your pelvis** under, as if bringing your belt buckle up towards your chin.
- Release the shoulders, jaw and breath.
- **Settle in softly:** nudge gently into position, then release. Engage only what's needed, release again, and maintain. When it's right, it feels light, almost floating.

## Conducting the exercise
- Keeping the pelvis tucked, shift your weight gently forward until you feel a stretch at the front of the hip of the kneeling leg.
- Stay there and breathe. Let the hip soften with each breath out.
- Don't chase the stretch. Let it come.

## What to watch for
- **Arching the lower back** instead of stretching the hip.
- **Leaning far forward** with the body.
- **The front knee** caving in.
- **Holding the breath.**

## Easier and harder versions
### Beginner
Hold a chair or wall for balance, with a small shift forward.

### Intermediate
No support, as above.

### Advanced
Reach the arm on the kneeling side up overhead and slightly across, keeping the ribs down.

The reach adds a second task to the stretch, and the ribs will want to flare. Keep them soft and let the breath do the work.

## Key points
- One knee down, pelvis gently tucked.
- Shift forward, no arching.
- Breathe and let it soften.

# The Method — principles and brand wording

Source: the owner's own words (voice note, 2026-10-23). Use this wording; don't embellish it with claims.

## The philosophy
Release the tension, the forcing and the control, and learn to love your body again. The homepage hints at this. Buyers get the full teaching.

## The philosophy in fuller words (owner, 2026-10-23)
- Release the tension and the forcing. Identify the spots where you're gripping and holding on.
- That holding stops the body accessing certain positions and fundamentals of movement, so you never truly trust yourself in a balanced position.
- It's practical and universal, **not "woo-woo"**.
- The flagship starts at the feet, but the effect goes up through the whole body. Even stage one (feet, toes and arches) affects how you move everywhere.

## Claims we won't make
- **"Helped thousands of people worldwide."** The owner asked for this, but confirmed it isn't true yet, so it's not used: it would be a misleading claim under ASA/CAP, CMA and DMCC Act rules. Use real member numbers and real testimonials once they exist.

## The six principles
1. **Recognise.** Become aware of tension, gripping, compensation and habitual patterns.
2. **Release.** Learn to let go of unnecessary muscular effort without losing useful stability.
3. **Reconnect.** Rediscover sensation, pressure distribution and controlled muscular engagement.
4. **Retrain.** Practise simple movements with improved coordination and awareness.
5. **Strengthen.** Progressively develop strength, balance and physical capability.
6. **Integrate.** Bring these improvements into everyday movement and more demanding tasks.

## Flagship order (Lower Body)
Feet, arches and toes → Achilles and calves → knees → hip stability.

## Who it's for / not for (owner's description, to be finalised in Step 2)
- **For:** people who've moved in compensating ways for years, older people who've not trained, and anyone wanting to rebuild movement from the ground up.
- **Not for, without medical clearance:** people with significant injuries (e.g. torn tissue) or unresolved conditions needing physio or medical care.

## Affiliates

- **Legal minimum, built in so the owner doesn't have to police it:**

  - Affiliates must not make medical claims.
  - This is required by UK advertising rules (CAP/CMA) and US FTC rules. The brand can be held partly responsible if affiliates mislead, so the terms let the owner remove an affiliate if needed.

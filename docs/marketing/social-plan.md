# Social media plan: Body Regen Kitchen and Body Regen Method

*Written 2026-10-26. Two brands, one system: free content gets attention, the free offer captures the email, the paid product converts, and referrals spread it.*

---

## Rules for both (non-negotiable)
- **No bots, fake accounts, bought followers or fake reviews.** It's against the platforms' rules and UK law (DMCC Act 2024), and it gets accounts banned.
- **Label paid or gifted posts `#ad`**, including influencers and beta testers who got free access.
- **Kitchen:** no "healthy", "nutritious" or weight-loss claims until the recipes are checked (see HANDOVER). Talk about **money, simplicity and real prices**. Never call it AI.
- **Method:** coaching, not treatment. Never say "fix", "cure", "heal" or "treat" pain or conditions. Talk about **tension, gripping, moving freely and feeling light**.
- **Every link carries a tracking code** so the reports show what works, e.g. `…/kitchen/free-week?ge=tiktok`, `?ge=insta`, `?ge=fb`, `?ge=pin`, one per influencer.
- **Handles:** try `@bodyregenkitchen` and `@bodyregenmethod` on every platform, and grab them all on day 1 even if you don't post there yet. *(Availability not checked.)*

## Body Regen Kitchen

**Who it's for:** households on a budget (families, mums, students, people feeling the cost of living).
**The message:** *"Tell us your budget. We'll plan your week, to the pound."*
**The hook:** the free week (no card).

### Best platforms to start (in this order)
1. **TikTok.** Budget food and "what £30 buys" videos are some of the most shared content there. It's the fastest way to reach strangers.
2. **Facebook (a page, plus posting value in budget, parenting and local community groups).** It's where UK families swap money-saving tips, and group shares spread through friends.
3. **Instagram Reels.** Re-post the TikToks. It's where people check you're real.
4. **Pinterest (from month 2).** People search it for "weekly meal plan" and "cheap family meals". Pins keep bringing visitors for months.

### Content (the 4 that work)
| Type | Example | How often |
|---|---|---|
| **Budget challenge** | "£25 feeds a family of 4 for a week: here's the plan" (show the plan, the shop, the receipt) | 3 a week |
| **One meal, real price** | "This costs £1.12 a portion" (30-60 sec cook) | 2 a week |
| **Shop with me** | Real supermarket trip with the shopping list, and the total at the till | 1 a week |
| **Member wins** | Real people's weekly spend (with permission) | when you have them |

- **Format:** vertical, 15-60 seconds. The number goes in the first second ("£30. Family of 4. 7 days."). Say the price out loud and put it on screen.
- **End every video the same way:** "Get your own week free: link in bio."
- **Bio:** "Your week of meals, planned to your budget 🛒 Free week 👇" with the link to `/kitchen/free-week?ge=…`

### First 30 days
- **Week 1:** set up all the accounts. Post 3 budget challenges (£20 single person, £35 couple, £50 family of 4). Join 10 UK budget and family Facebook groups and *help* there (answer questions) before you ever post a link.
- **Week 2:** daily posting. Start the "£X a day" series. Invite your 10-20 beta testers (beta code) and ask them to film their shop.
- **Week 3:** message 5-10 small budget/family creators (5k-50k followers) and offer free access plus their own commission link. They must use #ad.
- **Week 4:** check the reports. Which `ge` link brought free weeks, and which free weeks turned into members? Do more of the winner and drop the rest.

## Body Regen Method

**Who it's for:** adults (often 35+) who feel stiff, tense or "held", including sporty people and people who sit all day.
**The message:** *"Most of us were never taught how to move. We learned how to hold on."*
**The hook:** the free 3-day taster (`/taster`).

### Best platforms to start (in this order)
1. **Instagram Reels.** Short, calm demonstrations look good there, and it's where people discover coaches.
2. **YouTube (Shorts first, then longer videos).** People *search* YouTube for "foot exercises" or "balance exercises". Videos keep working for years, and longer ones build trust in you as the coach.
3. **Facebook.** The 40+ audience is strongest here. Re-post the Reels and share them in walking, running and active-ageing groups.
4. **TikTok (once you have a rhythm).** Repurpose the Reels.

### Content (the 4 that work)
| Type | Example | How often |
|---|---|---|
| **Try this now** | "Stand on one leg. Notice your toes gripping? Now let them go." (20 sec) | 3 a week |
| **The idea** | You to camera: "Why we start at the feet", "Control, then release" | 1 a week |
| **One exercise, done well** | Short foot / toe yoga, with the common mistakes | 1 a week |
| **Real people** | Before and after *in how they describe it* ("I feel lighter"), with permission, with no medical claims | when you have them |

- **Format:** calm, slow and real. Your voice, natural light, bare feet. The opposite of shouty fitness content, and that's what will make it stand out.
- **End every video the same way:** "3 free exercises, one a day: link in bio."
- **Bio:** "Release tension. Move from the ground up 🦶 Free 3-day taster 👇" with the link to `/taster?ge=…`

### First 30 days
- **Week 1:** set up the accounts. Film 10 short clips in one session (one per exercise idea) and post 3.
- **Week 2:** post daily (Reels and Shorts), plus one longer YouTube video: "Why your feet grip, and how to let go".
- **Week 3:** reach out to running clubs, Pilates and yoga teachers, and physio-adjacent creators. Offer the taster plus a commission link, and give gyms the partner codes.
- **Week 4:** check the reports. Taster sign-ups by `ge` link, and taster-to-buyer. Double down on the winner.

## Making the two brands help each other
- **Kitchen buyers see the Method** (the Kitchen page footer already does this). Later, a "Method + Kitchen" bundle *(not built, owner: later)*.
- **The same weekly filming session:** one day a week, film 5 Kitchen and 5 Method clips. Batch filming is what keeps you consistent.
- **One email list:** both free offers ask for an email (double opt-in), so you build one audience you own. Use it with Admin → Email campaigns.

## Each week, look at
1. **Reach:** views and follows by platform.
2. **Captures:** free weeks and taster sign-ups by `ge` link (your growth engine and the morning report).
3. **Sales:** members by link.
4. **Spread:** how many members share their code, once refer-a-friend is built.

Change one thing at a time. Give each test 2 weeks.

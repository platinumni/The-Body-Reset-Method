# Partner pitch: Body Regeneration Kitchen for organisations

Written 2026-10-28 (owner CH). The web page is `/kitchen/partners`; this file is the outreach email the owner sends **by hand** (no automated cold emails).

## Who to approach (types, not named contacts)
- **Housing associations:** the tenancy sustainment, financial inclusion or "money advice" team.
- **Councils:** the cost-of-living, household support or public health team.
- **Food support and advice charities:** local food banks, community pantries and advice centres. Approach them once there's a free or very low-cost option for them.
- **Employers:** HR or wellbeing leads, especially in sectors with lower pay.

Not DWP / Universal Credit: government departments don't promote paid commercial products or take commission.

## What's already built
- Partner codes (Admin → Access codes): the organisation pays monthly (invoiced from Stripe by hand), people join with email only and no card. There's a maximum number of places, and ending a code removes the access it gave.
- A results page per code: joined, active in the last 30 days, later sales. Counts only, no personal details.
- A free trial code for their staff to test it first (make a "beta"-type code).

## Still needed from the owner
- **Partner price** (shown as a placeholder on the page). Options: a flat monthly fee per organisation, or a price per place.
- A contact email (the page currently uses the contact form).
- Good recipe photos first (owner's choice: no launch until the photos are good).

## Email template (send by hand, personalise the first line)

Subject: Free budget meal plans for your [tenants / residents / clients / staff]

Hi [name],

I run Body Regeneration Kitchen, a UK meal planner built for tight budgets. Someone enters their weekly food budget, to the pound, and who they're feeding, and gets a week of breakfasts, lunches, evening meals and snacks that fits. It comes with simple recipes and a shopping list, priced from official UK shop prices for their region.

I'd like to offer it to the people [organisation] supports. You'd get your own code to share. They join with just an email: no card and no cost to them. Each month you'd see how many have joined and how many are using it, as numbers only. We never share anyone's details.

Could I set up a free trial code so your team can try it first? Here's what it looks like: [site]/kitchen/partners

Thanks,
[owner's name]
Body Regeneration Kitchen, part of The Body Regeneration Method

## Wording rules
- Say "estimated", "based on official UK shop prices, updated monthly". Never "live prices".
- No health, weight-loss or medical claims. Never call it AI.
- No made-up numbers (members, savings) until there are real ones from Admin → Stats.

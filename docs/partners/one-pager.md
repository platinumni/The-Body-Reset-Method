# Body Regeneration Kitchen: for your members, staff or tenants

**Healthy-eating meal plans built for any weekly budget, to the pound.**

## The problem
Food costs are one of the biggest pressures on household budgets. People want to eat well but don't know how to make the money stretch.

## What your people get
- They tell us their weekly food budget and who they're feeding. We plan a full week of simple home-cooked meals that fits.
- They get every recipe, a shopping list priced from **official UK shop prices for their region** (Office for National Statistics), updated monthly.
- Swaps for any meal, foods to leave out, the 14 main allergens left out on request, and a new plan every week.
- It works on any phone. No app to download.

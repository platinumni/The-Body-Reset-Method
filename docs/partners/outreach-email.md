**Short version (LinkedIn / Facebook message):**

Hi [name], I run Body Regeneration Kitchen: weekly meal plans built to any food budget, priced from official UK shop prices. I'm offering a few [employers / housing associations / clubs] a free one-month pilot for their people. Would that be useful for [organisation]? Happy to send a one-pager.

---

**Tips**
- Start with people you know, or local organisations (warm contacts reply far more often).
- Look for job titles with "wellbeing", "HR", "people", "tenant support" or "community".
- Follow up once after a week if there's no reply. Then leave it.
- Don't make health claims. Stick to budgets, simplicity and real prices.

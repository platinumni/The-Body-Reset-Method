# Licensing, attribution and intellectual property

Not legal advice. Flag anything uncertain to the owner.

## Recipes
- **Names:** `recipe-catalogue-2026-10-25.json` was supplied by the owner (140 concept names). Additions (ids 141-225) were written by Claude at the owner's request.
- **Full recipes** (ingredients, method, notes, taglines) were written from scratch by Claude for the owner, "in our own words": no copied cookbook text, no chef or restaurant brand names (owner instruction, 2026-10-27). They belong to the owner's business, so reuse in another of the owner's brands is the owner's call. **Confirm with the owner that Platinum Life may reuse them** (same owner, so expected yes).
- Status: **`draft_for_owner_review`**. Amounts, methods and allergens haven't been checked by a person. Platinum Life should keep the same caution, or have them checked before launch.
- Allergen tags are **auto-generated** from ingredient names: a filter, not a guarantee. Keep the "always check labels" notice. A nutritionist check is on the owner's to-do list before any health claims.

## Recipe photos (Pixabay)
- Licence: **Pixabay Content License** (https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/). Free for commercial use, **no attribution required**. You may not sell or redistribute the images on their own (unaltered), may not suggest endorsement by people shown, and identifiable trademarks or brands need care.
- Body Regen **downloads each image into its own R2** (never hotlinks) and records `source`, `source_id`, `page_url`, `photographer`, `licence` and `search` per photo. No visible credit is shown (allowed).
- **Platinum Life must not copy Body Regen's R2 images or rows.** Pick them again into Platinum Life's own storage, with its own records. Same licence, so it's allowed either way, but it keeps the two products separate and the records accurate. `photo-searches.json` (hand-written search terms) can be reused.
- Quality: Body Regen found about half the early auto-picks were wrong dishes. The current picker filters by tags, raw ingredients and duplicates, but **a person must check every photo** before launch.
- Pexels (paused new API keys in Oct 2026) and Unsplash (needs an application and attribution) are the alternatives.

**Wording rules** (owner decisions): say "estimated", "based on official UK shop prices, updated monthly". **Never "live prices".** Every plan states the source and month and that actual prices vary.

## Health and nutrition wording
- Plans are **maintain-weight**. For gaining or losing weight, refer to a GP, registered dietitian or registered nutritionist.
- No medical, disease, nutrition or health claims. It's not for medical conditions.
- Babies under 1 are excluded (NHS weaning guidance); there's a toddler choking and salt note.
- The calorie estimate is for adults only, worked out on the phone and never stored.

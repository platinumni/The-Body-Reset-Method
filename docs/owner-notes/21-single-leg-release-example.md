# Voice note 21 (owner, 2026-10-24): the single-leg release example. No changes yet; build at CH.
Applies to: the Release example on the method page (PRINCIPLES_DEPTH "Take standing on one leg..."), the six principles section in the course, and the single-leg balance exercise (conducting and advanced).
- The stacking goes further up the spine: ankle, knee, hip, then ribs, spine and head all stacked.
- The toe grip is there at first. That's the consciously competent bit: you're gripping at the toes, and you've got it.
- Then release even that. Trust the balance and coordination (wording: postural reflexes, balance reactions).
- You want the grip available whenever it's needed: the flexibility and adaptability to engage it only when necessary.
- When you lean left or right, you'll engage it too much. Then release again.
- Eventually the body naturally drops into the equilibrium point.

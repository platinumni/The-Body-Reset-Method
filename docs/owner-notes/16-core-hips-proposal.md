# Core & Hips: proposed exercise list (Claude, 2026-10-24). Awaiting owner approval; build at CH.
Owner brief: deep core first (warm-up and developmental positions), culminating in bird dogs, dead bugs, plank and side plank done "released but deep".
- Advanced options: yoga ball, and the cross dead bug (left knee into right elbow, other two limbs released). Correct term: **contralateral / cross-body control**.

## Part 1: Breath and deep-core foundations (warm-up)
1. 360° breathing (crocodile breath, lying face down)
2. 3-month supine (developmental back position)
3. 90/90 feet on the wall
4. Pelvic tilts and finding neutral
5. Heel slides and knee fall-outs
6. Side-lying breathing and reach
7. Quadruped rocking

## Part 2: Hips
8. 90/90 hip switches
9. Supported hip airplanes

## Part 3: Culmination
10. Dead bug (incl. the cross ball-press version)
11. Bird dog (incl. on a yoga ball)
12. Front plank (released first, the "squeeze" plank last)
13. Side plank

# Voice note 3 (owner, 2026-10-24): Exercise 2, Toe yoga (Stage 1, feet). Raw capture, build at CH.

## Starting position
- Almost exactly the same as the short foot exercise: seated on a hard chair, barefoot.
- **Emphasise spending time here.** Release tension everywhere, with everything straight and aligned as it should be.
- You may need to force or control yourself into position slightly at first. Once you're straight, release that area.
- Release the breath. Focus on breathing and being at equilibrium, a balanced stance point.
- Imagine the rib cage floating 360° as you breathe, fully loose. You might even be a little "wavy".

## Conducting the exercise
- Push the big toe into the ground and raise the other four toes off the ground. Hold for a couple of seconds, then go slowly down. Then up again, slowly.
- (Implied alternate: four toes down, big toe up. The owner mentions "same your four other toes" in advanced. Confirm at CH.)

## Levels
- **Basic:** just do the movement.
- **Intermediate:**
  - Consciously notice where you can release tension.
  - At first you'll push the big toe down far too hard. Reduce the pressure until you cross the point where the toe almost isn't touching, then re-engage. Find the sweet spot, while still raising the four toes as high as possible.
  - Watch for compensation: glutes tensing, ribs and spine twisting, forgetting to breathe.
- **Advanced:**
  - Count the pressure holds while continuing to breathe, without letting the counting sync with the breathing.
  - Count each time the big toe is down, and the same for the four other toes.
  - Still no over-tensing, over-gripping or over-exerting, even once the right spot is found.
  - Once the other tension (glutes, hips, spine) is gone and it's "just the toes operating", release another 5–10%. Feel where the toes release too much and want to come up. Find "that beautiful spot".
